# Brand

<p><img alt="keel mark" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MiladNalbandi/keel/main/assets/brand/keel-mark.svg" width="120"></p>

The mark is the front view of a hull with its keel, split down the middle. A keel is the part of a
ship you do not see. It keeps the ship upright and on course. keel does the same for an AI agent.

| | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| accent | `#7a5cff` | `#a48bff` |
| ink | `#131314` | `#f7f6f3` |
| paper | `#f7f6f3` | `#131314` |

The wordmark is JetBrains Mono ExtraBold in capitals with wide spacing, converted to outlines.

All files (SVG, PNG, app icons and the social preview) are in
[`assets/brand/`](https://github.com/MiladNalbandi/keel/tree/main/assets/brand), with usage rules in its README.
